description: >-
 This song relates the story of the 1876 Catalpa rescue from Fremantle prison in Western Australia. This involved the rescue of six men who had been imprisoned for their actions as part of the revolutionary Irish nationalist Fenian Brotherhood by comrades of theirs in America, who bought and commandeered the whaling ship *Catalpa*. This ship, after a short period of actual whaling in the Atlantic, was sailed to Western Australia by another Fremantle escapee, John Boyle O'Reilly, who sent a whaleboat to shore for the six convicts to board, then raised the US flag as they made their escape, thereby ensuring that the colonial police could not attack the Catalpa without potentially starting a war.

 The traditional version of this song refers to these prisoners as 'Fenians' throughout but, given the word's history as a derogatory term for Irish nationalists and the Irish more generally, we have here replaced it with 'prisoners'. We have also opted to use the traditional tune for "The Catalpa", but have made the addition of the 'away, away' portions and repeated final lines to make it more singalong-friendly.

A noble whale ship and commander\
Was called the *Catalpa*, they say,\
She came down to Western Australia\
And she took six poor prisoners away\
***Away away, away away\
She took six poor prisoners away.***

>***So come all you screws, warders and jailers,\
And remember Perth regatta day:\
Take care of the rest of your prisoners\
Or the whalers will steal them\
Away away, away away\
The whalers will steal them away.***

Seven long years they had served here\
And seven long more had to stay,\
For defending their country, Old Ireland\
For that they were banished away.\
***Away away, away away\
For that they were banished away.***

You kept them in Western Australia\
Till their hair on their heads would turn grey,\
But the Yanks from the States of America\
Came out here and stole them away.\
***Away away, away away\
They came here and stole them away.***

>***So come all you screws, warders, and jailers...***

And all the Perth boats are a-racing\
And making short tack for the spot,\
But the Yank she tacked into Fremantle\
And took the best prize of the lot.\
***Away away, away away\
And took the best prize of the lot.***

Then the *Georgette*, armed with bold warriors,\
Went out the poor Yanks to arrest.\
But she runs up her star-spangled banner,\
Saying, "You'll not arrest me, I guess."\
***Away away, away away\
Saying, "You'll not arrest me, I guess."***

>***So come all you screws, warders and jailers,\
And remember Perth regatta day:\
Take care of the rest of your prisoners\
Or the whalers will steal them\
Away away, away away\
The whalers will steal them away.***

And they've landed all safe in America\
And there they are able to cry,\
"Hoist up the green flag in The Shamrock,\
Hurrah for old Eire we'll die!"\
***Away away, away away\
"Hurrah for old Eire we'll die!"***

So remember those six prisoners colonial\
And sing all of these verses with skill\
And remember the whaleship that stole them away\
And the home that they left on the hill\
***Away away, away away,\
The home that they left on the hill***


>***So come all you screws, warders and jailers,\
And remember Perth regatta day:\
Take care of the rest of your prisoners\
Or the whalers will steal them\
Away away, away away\
The whalers will steal them away.***
