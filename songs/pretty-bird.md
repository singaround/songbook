I signed aboard a clipper ship\
To sail the ocean blue\
I left my love in London Town\
And swore that I'd be true\
And once we'd raised the anchor\
I was talking with the crew\
A bunch of nosey bastards\
And they all wanted to know --

>**Do you have a girlfriend?\
Have you got a wife?\
I bet you've got some pretty bird\
With whom you spend your life\
Tell us all about her\
While we're sailing on the sea\
And if you get back home again\
Give her one from me!**

Once the sails were hoisted\
Their questions came apace\
"Do you have a photograph\
So we can see her face?\
Does she have Mae West's bristols?\
Does she have ruby lips?\
And does she dance with all the boys\
Who go to sea in ships?"

>(they asked me) **Do you have...**

I told them that I had a love\
I wouldn't share their name\
And I didn't want their questions\
But they asked them just the same\
Then when were in sight of shore\
(we'd been two months at sea)\
I called the big old first mate\
And I gave my answer free:

>**No, I don't have a girlfriend\
I ain't got no wife\
But there's a strong and handsome man -\
With him I spend my life\
He's the one I dream of\
When I sail the sail the ocean blue\
And when we get back home again\
I'll give him one from you!**
